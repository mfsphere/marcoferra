Applications

Three small applications run this site's scheduling. Each is operated by Marco Ferra alone, and each page below states exactly what it does and what it reads.

What runs here

  • Booking

    Checks availability and creates the meeting you book on this site, with a video link.

  • Calendar invites

    Sends the invitation for every meeting on the calendar as mail aligned to this domain.

  • Domain verification

    Proves ownership of marcoferra.com to Google. Reads no personal data.
