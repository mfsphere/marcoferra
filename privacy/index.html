Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Marco Ferra, in Portugal, is the controller of your data. Write to hello@marcoferra.com.

What is collected, and why

  • Browsing: no cookies, analytics or trackers. Your IP address is used to deliver the pages and, for up to an hour, to stop abuse of the booking service (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Booking: your name, email address, time zone, the time you chose and any message, including a Role Conversation's brief. They are used to hold the meeting and send its emails (contract, Art. 6(1)(b)). You need to give your name and email address to book.
  • Paid sessions: the payment record, kept as Portuguese tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(c)). Card details go to Stripe only.
  • Where you came from: if your link named where it was shared, that name is kept with your booking, to learn which channels work (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it

GitHub and Amazon Web Services (hosting and the booking service), Google (calendar and Meet), Stripe (payments), Maileroo (email) and Proton (replies). Where they process data outside the EEA, they do so under an adequacy decision, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or Standard Contractual Clauses. Calendar data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Nothing is sold or used for marketing.

How long it is kept

Bookings until you ask for them to be deleted. Payment records for 10 years, as tax law requires. IP addresses for an hour at most in the booking service, and for a short time in the hosts' logs.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form, at hello@marcoferra.com. You may also complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the CNPD.
