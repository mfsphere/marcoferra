Payment

A paid session is paid in advance on Stripe's secure checkout page, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, MB WAY, or PayPal. The time you chose is held for 30 minutes while you pay. If the payment is not completed, the hold ends and nothing is charged. Card and account details are entered on Stripe's page only and never reach this site. If a payment arrives after its time was released or taken, it is refunded in full.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel any session from the link in its invitation. A paid session is refunded in full, automatically and to the way you paid, when you cancel it before it starts and either within 14 days of booking it or at least 24 hours before it starts. If Marco Ferra has to cancel a session, you are told by email and refunded in full.
